Film Collection helps you quickly explore movies without extra navigation or complicated menus. The main page shows film cards with the most important information, so you can compare films at a glance and choose what to open next.

The favorites feature lets you create a personal selection of films. You can mark interesting movies while browsing and return to them later instead of searching through the whole collection again.

The site is simple and focused: search, open details, check key information, and save films to favorites. It is useful for keeping a small movie list organized and easy to use.
